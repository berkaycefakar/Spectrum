Spectrum — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Spectrum is a music logging app. This policy explains what it collects, why, and how to get rid of it. Spectrum does not sell your data, does not track you across other apps or websites, and contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

What is collected

DataWhy
Email addressTo create and sign you into your account. Never shown to other users.
Username, bio, profile photoYour public profile.
Your logs: songs, albums and artists you rate, your written reviews, the colour you pickThe core function of the app.
Who you follow and who follows youTo build your feed.
Reports and blocks you fileSafety and moderation.

Your profile and your logs are public. Any signed-in user can see your username, photo, bio, ratings and reviews. Your email address is not public.

What is not collected

  • No location data.
  • No contacts or address book.
  • No advertising identifiers, no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No third-party analytics.

Where the data lives

Account data and logs are stored with Supabase, which hosts the database and authentication for Spectrum. If you sign in with Apple or Google, that provider confirms your identity to us and gives us your email address; we do not receive your password.

Song, album and artist information, artwork and previews come from Apple Music (MusicKit). Your use of Apple Music is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. Spectrum does not send your listening history to Apple.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your profile, all your logs and reviews, your follows, your blocks, your uploaded photo and your login record. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

You can also delete individual logs at any time without deleting your account.

Children

Spectrum is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
