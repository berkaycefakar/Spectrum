Anything you write — reviews, your username, your bio — stays yours. By posting it you allow Spectrum to display it inside the app to other users. Your profile and your logs are public: anyone with an account can see them.

3. Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behaviour

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users on Spectrum. Accounts that post such content are removed, usually within 24 hours of a report.

You agree not to post content that is:

4. Reporting and blocking

Every review in the app can be reported: press and hold it, or use the ⋯ button on the card. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours. You can also block any user from the same menu — blocking hides all of their content from you and removes any follow relationship in both directions. Blocked users are listed under Settings → Blocked Users and can be unblocked at any time.

Spectrum additionally filters written reviews for offensive language automatically. Reported content and the accounts that posted it may be removed without notice.

5. Music data

Song, album and artist information, artwork and 30-second previews come from Apple Music via MusicKit. Spectrum does not host or stream full songs. An Apple Music subscription is not required.

6. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your logs, your reviews, your follows and your profile. It cannot be undone.
