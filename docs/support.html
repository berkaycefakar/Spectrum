Common questions

Search returns nothing.
Spectrum needs permission to use Apple Music to look anything up. Go to Settings → Spectrum on your iPhone and turn on Media & Apple Music. An Apple Music subscription is not required — search, artwork and 30-second previews all work without one.
Do I need an Apple Music subscription?
No. Spectrum plays 30-second previews, which are free.
How do I report a review or block someone?
Press and hold any review card, or tap the ⋯ button on it, then choose Report or Block. Blocking hides that person completely and unfollows in both directions. Blocked users are listed under Settings → Blocked Users if you want to undo it.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile, every log and review you wrote, your follows and your login. It is permanent and cannot be undone.
I forgot my password.
Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen. You'll get an email with a link that opens Spectrum and lets you set a new one. If you are already signed in, use Settings → Change Password.
Who can see my logs?
Everyone with an account. Profiles, ratings and reviews are public. Your email address is not.
